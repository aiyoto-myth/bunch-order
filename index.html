How it counts

Stems needed = for every piece, how many you're making times the stems in its recipe, added up across the events in the order.

Buffer: stems needed plus your breakage % (10% unless you change it), rounded up. A flower can have its own % (garden roses often need more).

On hand: stems already in your cooler or your own field come off before rounding. With several events, the earliest event uses them first.

Bunches: what's left, divided by stems per bunch (or per box), rounded up. Flowers sold by the stem are counted in stems, never as bunches.

Extra: what rounding up leaves you beyond recipes and buffer. Only these stems are suggested for adding to pieces (only pieces with 3 or more stems of that flower, so a boutonniere is never doubled). Your buffer stays your buffer.

Each event's flower cost: every flower's cost is split between events by how many of its stems each event uses, so buffer and rounding are inside each event's cost. The event costs add up to the order.

Together vs one at a time: each event ordered on its own (in date order, cooler stems going to the earliest), versus all of them in one order.

Bunch sizes marked "guess" are our starting guess, not your wholesaler's. Your data stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
